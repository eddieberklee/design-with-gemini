# Cell 08 — Webcam Hand & Spatial Gesture Cursor (`cells/hand-gesture/`)

## 1. Intent & Architecture

`cells/hand-gesture/` implements **Cell 08** on the primary grid and its in-page **9-Cell Spatial Hand & Gesture Studio**, turning a standard RGB webcam (`@mediapipe/tasks-vision@1.2.3`) into a low-latency spatial cursor supporting **hand raise recognition, 2D position tracking, pinch (click/drag/zoom), continuous wrist rotation, and vertical up/down control**:

- **Primary Grid Cell (`#cell-hand-gesture` in `cells/hand-gesture/ui.js:mountHandGestureCell`)**: a shared `.ui-tile` (class added in JS; `index.html` is untouched). Header: index `08`, title "Hand Cursor", status pill (`setPortalPill`), `Cam` and `Sim` (`ui-btn--sm`, `aria-pressed`, rust `data-tone="attention"` while running or while a camera request is pending, `syncPortalToggles`), and `Studio ↗` as a quiet button (no black primary on this tile). `Cam` / `Sim` activate the floating hand cursor across the **entire main page** (clicks and V-scroll); `Escape` on the grid turns it off (their tips show the Esc keycap while running). Body: the hero stage (`.hg-portal-stage`, role button, opens the Studio) holding one centred 4:3 camera frame (`.hg-mini-frame`, sized with container units so the mirrored video and the `160 × 120` hero SVG share coordinates; strokes are `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`), which surfaces three visual tracking layers inside the stage while detected gestures appear once in the `Gesture` readout and header pill:
  1. **Hand & Pose Tracking**: 21-joint mirrored skeleton, thumb–index pinch gap line, metacarpal anchor dot, and `200 ms` arming dwell progress ring (`miniAnchorRing`).
  2. **Visual & Numeric Tolerances**: dashed `ROI .22–.78` cursor-area box (`CLUTCH MODE` when relative clutch is selected), dotted `KBD <.86` keyboard-exclusion horizon + tinted exclusion band, and `SPAN ≥.055 · FACE ≥+.22` gate markers that highlight in `--ui-accent` when violated.
  3. **Mini Screen-Cursor Monitor Inset (`.hg-mini-screen`)**: top-right miniature screen viewport displaying an 18-sample cursor motion trail (`.hg-mini-cursor-trail`), live cursor crosshair/dot (`.hg-mini-cursor-dot`), and `14 px` click-lock anchor ring (`.hg-mini-cursor-lock`) without a redundant `CURSOR` text label inside the stage.
  Under the hero sits one quiet row of `Cursor / Pinch <.28 / Twist ±3.5° / Gesture` label + value pairs (`.ui-hide-compact`), where `Pinch <.xx` tracks the live Schmitt slider (`syncSchmittMarks`) and `Gesture` is driven by zero-GC `classifyGesture()` (with a `950 ms` hold latch on discrete `PINCH TAP` clicks, reporting `STANDBY`, `ARMING`, `OPEN PALM`, `PINCH TAP`, `PINCH LOCK`, `PINCH DRAG`, `TWIST`, `SCROLL ↓` / `SCROLL ↑`, `2H`, `FIST CLUTCH`, `COAST`, and gate rejections). Foot: `.ui-prose` instruction at rest (`PORTAL_HINT` via `setPortalStatus`, with dynamic tolerance tooltip `portalHintTip()` synced with `engine.config.pinchEngage` in `syncSchmittMarks()` and `resetPortalPreview()`) switching at 30 Hz when active to live threshold-consistent gate readings + vertical scroll velocity (`Gates: span .14≥.055 · face +.94≥.22 · y .50<.86 · scroll 0px/s`). Pill labels (7-character width reserved): `Off` / `IDLE` grey; `WAITING` (camera permission), `LOADING` (model), `NO CAM` (failure, tip = error text), `ARMING`, `COAST`, `ARMED`, `PINCHED` rust. Size steps: < 320 px the pill collapses to its dot (label visually hidden, still announced), `Studio ↗`, the readout row and the foot hide while the hero SVG keeps the hand, tolerances, and cursor inset legible; < 240 px `Cam` and `Sim` hide too (header + hero only; the stage still opens the Studio). Runs **zero** `requestAnimationFrame` loops while `Off` (`TRACKER_MODE.IDLE`) or while the tab is hidden (`ui.js:shouldRunLoop`), and gates synthetic figure-8 `autoOrbit` on `!prefersReducedMotion()`. `Cam` reports `Requesting camera…` / `Loading hand model…` while pending; a failure leaves the tile in `NO CAM` with a message to press `Sim` (`IDLE` fallback).
- **Secondary 9-Cell `.grid` Studio (`#hand-gesture-page`)**: `ui-studio-bar` (`.hg-studio-bar`, a grid: one row ≥ 1280 px, two ≥ 760 px, three below) with index `08`, "Hand Studio", the input `ui-seg` (Synth / Tour / Webcam / Pointer), `Map`, five live tolerance + gesture `ui-pill`s (`setGate`: `SPAN .14≥.055`, `FACE +.94≥.22`, `KBD .50<.86`, `IDLE 200ms` / `DWELL` / `ARMED`, and detected gesture from `classifyGesture()`; idle grey, pass ink via `data-state="pass"`, fail or active rust; no green because a Sim pass is not a real pass), FPS readout (hidden < 560 px), `Keys` and `Close` (last, Esc). `#hand-gesture` History hash and View Transition `hg-stage-portal` as before. Opening scrolls to the top and focuses `Close`; closing restores the main-grid scroll position and focuses `Studio ↗`, or the stage when `Studio ↗` is hidden at compact size. Each instrument is `section.cell.ui-tile.hg-wb-cell` built by `instrument()`: index, sans title, quiet meta readout (`.hg-cell-metric`), body, and a shared `.ui-tile-foot.ui-tile-foot--keep.hg-cell-foot` (kept down to 240 px instead of hiding below 320 px; instruments are ~273 px wide at a 900 px page). The nine instruments:
  1. **`01` Hand Rig**: mirrored webcam feed + 21-joint overlay, live gesture HUD + tolerance captions (`CURSOR AREA .22–.78`, `KEYBOARD ZONE ≥.86`, live `span · face · y`), pointer-driven `SyntheticHandRig`, pose buttons (Hand, Pinch, Scroll, Flip, 2 Hands, Orbit) that wrap to two rows when narrow.
  2. **`02` Smoothing Scope**: 2D cursor path + raw-vs-`OneEuroFilter2D` trace and speed, with jitter attenuation in dB in the meta readout.
  3. **`03` Pinch Pads**: 3×3 practice pads + Schmitt bar (`engage < 0.28`, `release > 0.40`, `14 px` lock); the marks follow the Pinch slider (`syncSchmittMarks`).
  4. **`04` Twist Dial**: multi-turn (`±720°`) dial from unwrapped wrist twist; `Angle: Fused / Knuckles / Wrist` picks the rotation source.
  5. **`05` Transform Stage**: 1-hand pinch-drag + twist card, 2-hand zoom + turn.
  6. **`06` Pinch Fader**: pinch anywhere in the panel to grab (`-∞..+6 dB`), peak hold, ARIA vertical slider.
  7. **`07` Scroll Tape**: 28-row tape for the two-finger scroll pose or isolated `Flick ↓` / `Flick ↑` momentum, `0.94` decay, `< 18 px/s` row detents, listbox keys (`ArrowUp`/`ArrowDown`/`Home`/`End`).
  8. **`08` Signal Chain**: tap (`< 14 px`) to bypass, pinch-drag (`>= 14 px`) to reorder. Display only: tracking ignores it (the tips say so).
  9. **`09` Tolerances**: sliders (Cutoff and Beta of the 1€ filter, Pinch at, Sim jitter), live gate tolerance readings (`Span ≥.055`, `Face ≥+.22`, `Height <.86`), `Chiral` and `Defaults`.
- **Colours**: SVG attributes and canvas drawing read `PAL` / `CAN` in `ui.js`, built once from the `--ui-` tokens (`getComputedStyle`, not per frame): ink for the hand and instruments, `--ui-accent` for live (pinch, drag, two hands, scroll) and rejected (back of hand, keyboard zone) states. The page cursor ring and tag follow the same rule; the tag text names the state.
- **Tooltips**: every control, status pill, gate, meta readout and unit-less value has a `data-tip` (`ui/tooltip.js`). State-dependent tips are rewritten where the state changes: `syncPortalToggles`, `setPortalPill`, `setPortalStatus` (error text), `syncMapChiralUi` (Map and Chiral, also called by `Defaults`), `setGate`. No `title` attributes remain.

## 2. Design & Technical Decisions (`Protocol B` Best-of-3 Synthesis)

Three parallel prototypes (*Prototype A: Swiss Instrument Grid*, *Prototype B: Spatial Indirect Fluid UI*, and *Prototype C: Kinetic Studio Workbench*) were evaluated by an independent Taste & Kinematics Critic (`44.0 / 50` winner: *Kinetic Studio Workbench*):

1. **Zero-GC `Float64Array(63)` Core + `ResizeObserver` Rect Caching (`kinematics.js:KinematicEngine`, `ui.js:refreshRectCache`)**:
   - *Won*: Pre-allocated `HandSlot` and `CandidateScratch` typed buffers plus cached `Float32Array` bounding boxes (`0` `getBoundingClientRect()` reads or SVG `innerHTML` reparses inside the 60fps `onFrame` loop).
   - *Rejected*: Per-frame object allocations, interleaved DOM rect reads/style writes, and placing a secondary spring in series after `OneEuroFilter2D` (which added sluggish cursor overshoot in *Prototype B*).
2. **Rigid Metacarpal Anchor + Pre-Pinch Brake + `14 px` Click-Lock (`kinematics.js:KinematicEngine._updateSlotFromSample`)**:
   - *Won*: Anchoring `(x, y)` at `0.50·L5 + 0.30·L9 + 0.20·L0` decouples the pointer from `INDEX_TIP (L8)`, which dips `25–45 mm` downward during natural thumb-index flexion (*Heisenberg Effect*). Combined with `0.08×` pre-pinch closing velocity damping and a `14 px` click-lock deadzone, taps fire with `0.0 px` slip.
   - *Rejected*: Raw `INDEX_TIP` tracking and `atan2(L8 - L4)` pinch-tip rotation (whose lever arm collapses to `0` at pinch contact, causing `±90°` angular noise).
3. **Fused Rigid Rotation + Switchable Absolute ROI / Relative Fist-Clutch (`kinematics.js:ROTATION_SOURCE`, `kinematics.js:MAPPING_MODE`)**:
   - *Won*: Blending `0.72·knuckleBar (L17-L5) + 0.28·wristLever (mid(L4,L8)-L0)` via branch-free `unwrapAngleDelta`, paired with both `[0.22, 0.78]` interior ROI mapping and closed-fist Relative Clutch trackpad ratcheting (grafted from *Prototype B*).

## 3. Hazards, Invariants & Edge Cases

- **Chiral 3D Normal Sign on Unmirrored Video (`kinematics.js:computePalmFacingCosFlat`)**: MediaPipe's handedness classifier assumes selfie-mirrored input, so on raw unmirrored `<video>` a physical Right palm facing the camera yields `(L5-L0)×(L17-L0)` `nz < 0` with label `'Left'` (`sign = -1 → +1`), and a physical Left palm yields `nz > 0` with label `'Right'` (`sign = +1 → +1`). Never invert `sign` without checking both chiralities (`kinematics.test.js` guards both).
- **Normalized-Unit `1€` `beta` Scaling (`kinematics.js:DEFAULT_CONFIG`)**: `OneEuroFilter2D` operates on `[0, 1]` ROI coordinates (`beta = 0.85`, `dCutoff = 1.8`), **not** pixel coordinates (`~0.0005`). Using pixel-scale `beta` constants on `[0, 1]` coordinates starves velocity adaptation and causes `~130 ms` sweep lag.
- **Non-Finite & Boundary Quarantine (`kinematics.js:clamp`, `kinematics.js:copyLandmarksToFlat`, `kinematics.js:ScopeRingBuffer`)**: Any frame containing `NaN`/`±Infinity` coordinates is dropped before touching `OneEuroFilter2D` or `document.elementFromPoint(x, y)`; `clickBreakoutPx = 0`, `x = 0` viewport-edge lock origins, and `candidateCount > 4` are all guarded against zero-division and out-of-bounds access.
- **Camera Teardown, Stream Quarantine & Coalescing (`hand-tracker.js:HandTrackerController.enableWebcam`, `hand-tracker.js:HandTrackerController.teardown`)**: `_sessionEpoch` increments on mode switches, `Escape` / `Close` navigation, tab hide, and `pagehide` (`tracker.teardown()`). Acquired streams are guarded both in `_pendingStream` and an attempt-local tracker so WASM/CDN failures or stale epochs stop only the stale attempt's tracks without disturbing any newer active stream, mid-`play()` `setVideoElement()` handoffs (`miniVideo → wbVideo`) tolerate the superseded element's `AbortError`, and `_pendingWebcamPromise` clears before `_emitStatus()` / `_enterFallback()` so `isWebcamStarting` is `false` inside `onStatusChange`.
- **Visibility (`ui.js:suspendCameraForHide`)**: Hiding the tab stops the camera (`IDLE` on the grid, `SYNTH` in the Studio) and cancels the rAF loop; showing the tab again restarts only a camera that the hide suspended (`cameraSuspendedByVisibility`).
- **Clock, Coordinate & Twist Reset + Primary-Hand Promotion (`kinematics.js:KinematicEngine.resetClock`, `kinematics.js:KinematicEngine.resetAll`, `kinematics.js:HandSlot.resetPosition`, `kinematics.js:KinematicEngine._promoteSecondaryIfPrimaryLost`)**: `resetClock()` zeroes `_lastStepSec` on standby/mode switches so the first resumed frame never skips the `200 ms` arming dwell; `resetAll()` and `resetSlot()` reset `cursorU/V` and `rawU/V` to `0.5` (while transient dropout `HandSlot.reset()` preserves `RELATIVE_CLUTCH` ratcheting); normal pinch release zeroes `twistBreakoutSign` and `twistTotalDeg` alongside `twistBrokenOut` so `OPEN PALM` never shows stale twist excursion. When slot 0 resets while slot 1 is tracked, slots swap so the surviving hand drives the cursor seamlessly.
- **Schmitt Band, Auto Tour Reset & Latch Hygiene (`kinematics.js:SyntheticHandRig.resetPosture`, `ui.js:takeManualRigControl`, `ui.js:onFrame`, `ui.js:clearSpatialLatches`, `ui.js:classifyGesture`, `ui.js:swapDomToMain`)**: Effective release is `max(pinchRelease, pinchEngage + 0.04)` to prevent alternate-frame click flapping, and `resetPosture()` clears leftover 2-hand/pinched poses when leaving `Auto Tour` or exiting Studio. `onFrame` re-checks `shouldRunLoop()` and view/mode state immediately after `dispatchMainGridGestures` / `dispatchSecondaryGestures` so a spatial `PINCH TAP` on `Sim`, `Cam`, or `Close` never overwrites the resting `Off` / `STANDBY` state; `clearSpatialLatches()` and `clearGestureHold()` run on mode switches, tab hide, and `pagehide`; `classifyGesture` clears its `950 ms` `PINCH TAP` hold latch whenever disarmed or superseded by another active gesture; `swapDomToMain` calls `tracker.setMode(IDLE)` before `tracker.setVideoElement(miniVideo)`; `endPuck(cancelled)` skips bypass toggling on `pointercancel` and `blur`.
- **Threshold-Safe Formatting & Responsive Studio Bar (`ui.js:fmtGateGe`, `ui.js:fmtGateLt`, `ui.js:fmtDot`, `ui.js:fmtSignedDot`, `hand-gesture.css`)**: `fmtGateGe` / `fmtGateLt` clamp cent rounding across gate thresholds so failing values (`0.219 < 0.22`) never round up to pass (`FACE +.21≥.22`) and passing values (`0.859 < 0.86`) never round up to fail (`KBD .85<.86`), while `roundCents` eliminates `-0` (`fmtSignedDot(-0.001) === '+.00'`). `.hg-gates` reserves `14ch` on the facing pill (`FACE +1.00≥.22`), and `.hg-studio-ctl` uses `flex-wrap: wrap` so `320 px` viewports never crush `.ui-seg` button labels.
- **Multi-Portal Hash, `ViewTransition` & SVG `class` (`ui.js:runPortalTransition`, `ui.js:otherSecondaryPageVisible`, `ui.js:svgEl`)**: Synchronous `targetOpen` + monotonic `vtSeq` deduplicate paired `popstate` + `hashchange` events, `.catch()` guards `vt.ready`, `vt.updateCallbackDone`, and `vt.finished` against `AbortError`, `crossCellHash` + `otherSecondaryPageVisible()` keep `main.grid` hidden and preserve sibling focus on cross-portal jumps, and `svgEl` writes `className` as `class` so `.hg-mini-svg` and `.hg-spatial-chord` match.
- **Tape Row Pitch, Blink Detent Quantization & Isolated Flick Momentum (`hand-gesture.css`, `ui.js:dispatchSecondaryGestures`)**: `.hg-tape-row` stays single-line (`25 px` pitch) at every width, with an explicit `13px` line-height (`normal` gave 22 px rows with the `--ui-mono` stack), `< 18 px/s` row detents enforce `Math.sign(gap) * Math.max(1, Math.abs(gap) * 0.22)` above `1.5 px` so Blink `< 0.5 px` `scrollTop` quantization never stalls `2 px` short of `25 px` multiples, and `Flick ↓` / `Flick ↑` button velocity (`c7.buttonVelPx`) is isolated from Cell 06 hand `V-Scroll`.
- **Frozen-Scope Snapshot & Render Cache (`ui.js:drawCell02Scope`, `ui.js:updateSpatialReticle`, `ui.js:setAttr`)**: `Hold Trace` snapshots held locus coordinates and `ScopeRingBuffer` typed arrays so window resizes redraw the exact held trace without mutating `trailU/V`, while hoisted `drawScopePosTrace` / `drawScopeSpeedTrace`, cached `tunerInputs` and per-frame `dialogOpen`, `miniBoneKey` / `miniCursorKey`, and `reticleCache` / `setAttr` diffing avoid redundant per-frame allocations and DOM attribute writes.
- **Dual `file://` and `http://localhost` Compatibility**: All local scripts load as classic UMD `<script defer>` files without ES-module `file://` CORS failures; if `getUserMedia` or CDN WASM is unavailable offline, `Synth Rig`, `Auto Tour`, and `Pointer` modes keep all 9 cells fully interactive.

## 4. How to Run & Verify

- **Automated kinematics, camera-lifecycle & portal unit suite**:
  `timeout 60 node --test cells/hand-gesture/kinematics.test.js`
- **Syntax check across all cell scripts**:
  `node --check cells/hand-gesture/kinematics.js && node --check cells/hand-gesture/hand-tracker.js && node --check cells/hand-gesture/ui.js && node --check cells/hand-gesture/kinematics.test.js`
- **Live browser & webcam verification**:
  Serve repo root (`python3 -m http.server 8000`), open `http://localhost:8000`, click **`Cam`** on **Cell 08** (to drive the cursor across the main grid) or click **`Studio ↗`** (`http://localhost:8000/#hand-gesture`) to test all 9 gesture instruments.
- **Camera paths without a camera**: In DevTools, replace `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia` with a function that rejects `new DOMException('x', 'NotAllowedError')` (denied path) or resolves `canvas.captureStream()` (granted path), and block `*cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@mediapipe*` to exercise model-load failure; verify every returned track ends in `readyState === 'ended'` after `Cam` off, Studio close, tab hide, `pagehide`, or load failure.
- **Hidden-tab automation**: rAF does not run in a background tab and the loop also stops when `document.hidden`; to drive frames, stub `document.hidden` to `false` and `requestAnimationFrame` with a manual pump before load. Stub `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia` too (a never-settling promise shows `WAITING`, a `NotAllowedError` rejection shows `NO CAM`): Auto Tour pinches by itself and the cursor `.click()`s whatever it is over. After a viewport resize, wait for the `ResizeObserver` and pump again, or the Studio canvases show blank (the scope black: `alpha: false`) until the next frame.
- **UI checks**: tape pitch `rows[1].offsetTop - rows[0].offsetTop === 25`; no `[title]` in the tile, Studio or overlay; no element outside its tile at 1600 / 900 / 390 / 360 / 320 px and with Motion's size slider at min and max.
